Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • You choose a nickname. If you're online, it appears on the public leaderboard with your furthest level and your number of deaths.
  • For the leaderboard, the app signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase, which gives your install a random ID.
  • The Android app uses Microsoft Clarity to see how the game is played (taps, screens and level events) so we can fix bugs and levels that are too hard. Nicknames are hidden from these recordings.
  • The app checks for game updates with Shorebird, so new levels arrive without a store update.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Trust Gravity ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Trust Gravity app (package com.game.dont_trust_gravity) and this website. By using them, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

a) Stored only on your device

The game saves a few things locally so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device:

  • The furthest level you have reached, and whether you have cleared every level
  • Your total number of deaths
  • Your sound on/off setting
  • Your nickname, and whether it has been reserved on the leaderboard

b) Leaderboard (Google Firebase)

When you're online, the game takes part in an online leaderboard run on Google Firebase:

DataWhyWho can see it
Your nickname (up to 14 characters)To show you on the leaderboard and keep every nickname uniquePublic: all players
Furthest level reached, total deaths, all-cleared flagTo rank playersPublic: all players
An anonymous Firebase user IDSo only you can update your own row and nicknameNot shown to players
Time of your last updateBasic record keepingNot shown to players

Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your nickname: it is public.

c) Gameplay analytics (Microsoft Clarity)

The Android app uses Microsoft Clarity, an analytics service from Microsoft. When you play, Clarity may automatically collect:

DataExamples
Session recordings & interactionsTaps, swipes and the game screens shown during a session. Nicknames on screen are masked.
Game eventsLevel started or completed, the cause of each death, sound turned on or off, a nickname being set (but not the nickname itself)
Device & app infoDevice model, operating system version, screen size, app version and language
Identifiers & networkA random, app-specific identifier for the install, and an IP address, which Microsoft may use to estimate your country or region

d) Game updates (Shorebird)

To deliver new levels and fixes without a store update, the app contacts Shorebird's update servers when it starts. This request includes technical information such as the app version, the installed update number, your device's platform and processor type, and an IP address. It does not include your nickname or progress.

e) What we don't collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

f) Permissions

The app uses Internet access (for the leaderboard, updates and analytics). It also uses your phone's built-in touch feedback to vibrate on button presses and deaths; this involves no data. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

3. How we use information

  • To save your progress and settings on your device
  • To run the leaderboard and keep nicknames unique
  • To understand how players move through the levels, for example where most people get stuck, so we can balance difficulty
  • To find and fix bugs, crashes and confusing controls
  • To deliver game updates

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

4. Sharing & third parties

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in and the leaderboard database): Firebase privacy and the Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Clarity (gameplay analytics): Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Shorebird (game updates): Shorebird privacy policy

Your nickname, level and death count are shared publicly with other players through the leaderboard. We share no other data, except when required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own policies when you download or update apps.

5. Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Leaderboard data (nickname, level, deaths) stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • Clarity analytics data is kept by Microsoft for a limited time under Clarity's retention policy and then deleted automatically.
  • Update-check logs are kept by Shorebird under its own retention policy.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Stay off the leaderboard: play offline. Without internet, nothing is uploaded.
  • Reset your progress: open Android Settings → Apps → Trust Gravity → Storage → Clear data.
  • Delete your leaderboard entry and nickname: email us with your nickname and we will remove it.
  • Stop all data collection: uninstall the app.
  • Access or deletion requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you. Contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

Trust Gravity is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nicknames are public, we remove nicknames that contain personal or inappropriate information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Leaderboard rules only let each player change their own entry. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep collection to a minimum.

9. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
