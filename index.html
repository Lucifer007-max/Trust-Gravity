About the game

A platformer that lies to you

Trust Gravity looks like a cute, simple platformer: run, jump, reach the door. Then the floor collapses, the door runs off and gravity turns upside down. 110 levels across four planets, each hiding a new trick you only find by falling for it.

🌱

Meet Sprout

A chubby apricot gumdrop with a two-leaf sprout and tiny legs. Sprout will die a lot. Sprout believes in you anyway.

🧠

Learn, die, laugh

Every trap is fair once you know it's there. Instant respawns and short levels keep you going for "just one more try".

💬

It talks back

Pick a nickname and the game roasts it. Every death comes with a fresh taunt, like "Skill issue." or "Medium rare." It's laughing with you. Mostly at you.

New planet, new physics

Each planet has its own gravity and its own ways to kill you. Clear one to unlock the next.

What's waiting for you

Every trick in the troll book

A few of the things that will betray you on the way to the exit.

🙃

Lying gravity

Gravity flips on the Moon, floats on Mars, crushes on Jupiter, and goes weightless in Saturn's helium pockets. Your jump is never the same twice.

🚪

Runaway doors

Exits that run away, parachute down, ride a rover over quicksand, orbit on a ring, or hide until you stand still. Plus plenty of fakes that kill you.

🔺

Hidden killers

Spikes that pop up, saws that spring out of the floor, lava from solid ground, and lightning with no warning. You'll learn where they are. The hard way.

🧱

Lying floors

Floors that crumble, ice that won't let you stop, quicksand dressed as rock, and clouds that vanish under your feet.

🪨

Things that chase you

Giant boulders faster than you, hunting thunderbolts, storms that drag you into their eye, and moonlets rolling in from space.

🏆

Beat everyone

An online leaderboard ranks every player by the furthest level reached, then by the fewest deaths. Your nickname is yours alone.

How to play

Simple controls. Unfair universe.

Move & jump

Use the on-screen arrows and the big jump button (your phone buzzes on every press). On a keyboard: ← → or A D, and Space / ↑.

Short hops

Hold jump briefly and let go for a short hop, perfect for landing on a nearby ledge instead of flying past it.

Pick your level

Open the level map to see every planet, replay any level you've cleared, and see what's next. Locked levels stay locked until you earn them.

Trust nothing

Every death teaches you the trick. Your progress saves on your device, and new levels and fixes arrive automatically, with no store update needed.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the game free?

Yes. Trust Gravity is free to download. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How many levels are there?

110: 25 on the Moon, 25 on Mars, 30 on Jupiter and 30 on Saturn. Each planet unlocks when you clear the one before it.

Which devices can I play on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed. You just pick a nickname. The levels play offline, and your progress is saved on your device. Internet is only used for the leaderboard, game updates and anonymous analytics.

How does the leaderboard work?

Players are ranked by the furthest level reached, then by the fewest deaths. Every nickname is unique, so nobody can copy yours. Your nickname, level and death count are visible to other players.

How do I get new levels?

Automatically. The game downloads updates in the background; when one is ready you'll see "New traps downloaded. Reopen the game!" Just close and reopen it.

Is it really that hard?

It's a troll game, so it's designed to surprise you. Every single level has been tested to be beatable, and once you know a trick it becomes fair.

What data does the game collect?

Your nickname and score for the leaderboard, an anonymous ID, and anonymous gameplay analytics to fix levels that are too hard. Nothing personal like your name, email or location. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Think you can beat all 110 levels?

The Moon, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn say no. Prove them wrong.
