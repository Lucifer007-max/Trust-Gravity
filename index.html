About the game

A platformer that lies to you

Trust Gravity looks like a cute, simple platformer: run, jump, reach the door. Then the floor collapses, the door runs off and gravity turns upside down. Each of the 25 levels hides a new trick, and you only find it by falling for it.

🌱

Meet Sprout

A chubby apricot gumdrop with a two-leaf sprout and tiny legs. Sprout will die a lot. Sprout believes in you anyway.

🧠

Learn, die, laugh

Every trap is fair once you know it's there. Instant respawns and short levels keep you going for "just one more try".

💬

It talks back

Each death comes with a fresh taunt, like "Skill issue." or "Gravity: 1. You: 0." The game is laughing with you. Mostly at you.

What's waiting for you

Every trick in the troll book

A few of the things that will betray you on the way to the exit.

🙃

Gravity flips

Step into a zone, some of them invisible, and you fall up. Some levels flip gravity on a timer. Some flip it every time you jump.

🚪

Runaway doors

Exits that run away, ride on platforms, parachute down, hide while you move, or swap places with a fake that kills you.

🔺

Sneaky spikes

Spikes that pop up, patrol, rain from the sky, or turn out to be made of paper. Watch the wind to tell which ones are real.

🧱

Lying floors

Bridges that crumble, floors that vanish under you, and a few random bridges that collapse each time you try.

⇄

Mirror controls

Left becomes right, mid-jump, over a spike pit. Your thumbs will need a moment. You won't get one.

🔨

Crushers & fake saves

Pistons that slam in rhythm, a crusher that hunts you, and a checkpoint that says "saved" but saves nothing.

How to play

Simple controls. Unfair world.

Move

Tap the left and right buttons on your phone. On a keyboard use ← → or A D.

Jump

Tap the jump button, or press Space / ↑. When gravity is flipped, you jump "down".

Reach the door

Get Sprout to the exit door to clear the level. Make sure it's the real door first.

Trust nothing

Every death teaches you the trick. Your progress is saved on your device, so you can always come back.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the game free?

Yes. Trust Gravity is free to download. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which devices can I play on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play. You can also try it in a desktop or mobile browser with the "Play in browser" button.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed. The game plays offline. Your level progress, total deaths and sound setting are saved only on your device.

Is it really that hard?

It's a troll game, so it's designed to surprise you. Every level has been tested to be beatable, and once you know a trick it becomes fair.

What data does the game collect?

The Android app uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how people play (for example, which levels are too hard). It doesn't ask for your name, email or location. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Think you can beat all 25 levels?

Gravity says no. Prove it wrong.
