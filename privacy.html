Privacy Policy

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Trust Gravity ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Trust Gravity mobile app (package com.game.dont_trust_gravity), the browser version of the game, and this website. By using them, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

a) Stored only on your device

The game saves a few settings locally so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device:

  • The furthest level you have reached, and whether you have cleared every level
  • Your total number of deaths
  • Your sound on/off setting

b) Usage and device data (Android app, via Microsoft Clarity)

The Android app uses Microsoft Clarity, an analytics service from Microsoft. When you play, Clarity may automatically collect:

DataExamples
Session recordings & interactionsTaps, swipes and the game screens shown during a session (the game shows no personal information on screen)
Game eventsLevel started or completed, the cause of each death, and sound turned on or off
Device & app infoDevice model, operating system version, screen size, app version and language
Identifiers & networkA random, app-specific identifier for the install, and an IP address, which Microsoft may use to estimate your country or region

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID.

c) Permissions

The app asks for only one Android permission: Internet access, which is used to send the analytics described above. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

3. How we use information

  • To save your progress and settings on your device
  • To understand how players move through the levels, for example where most people get stuck, so we can balance difficulty
  • To find and fix bugs, crashes and confusing controls
  • To improve the game over time

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

4. Sharing & third parties

Usage data from the Android app is processed by Microsoft Corporation as part of the Microsoft Clarity service. Microsoft handles this data under its own privacy terms; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

We share no other data, except when required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own policies when you download or update apps; see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Clarity analytics data is kept by Microsoft for a limited time under Clarity's retention policy and then deleted automatically.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Reset your progress: open Android Settings → Apps → Trust Gravity → Storage → Clear data.
  • Stop all data collection: uninstall the app.
  • Access or deletion requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you. Contact us using the details below. Because we don't collect names or emails, we may need details such as your device model and the approximate dates you played to find your data.

7. Children's privacy

Don't Trust Gravity is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to Microsoft Clarity is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep collection to a minimum, which keeps the risk low.

9. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. The browser version of the game saves your progress in your browser's local storage, on your own computer. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
